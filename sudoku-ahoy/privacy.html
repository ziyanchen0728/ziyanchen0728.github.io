Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Sudoku Ahoy handles information when you use the app. Sudoku Ahoy is a logic puzzle game. We do not operate an account system and we do not ask you to provide a name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location in order to play.

2. Gameplay data

Gameplay information such as puzzle progress, achievements, settings, and preferences may be stored locally on your device so the app can remember your progress. We do not intentionally upload this local gameplay data to our own servers.

3. Advertising and third-party SDK data

Sudoku Ahoy uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. Depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, and Google’s services, Google and its advertising partners may process data such as approximate location inferred from IP address, device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction data, crash logs, and performance data.

These data may be used for third-party advertising, analytics, diagnostics, fraud prevention, service operation, and performance improvement. Sudoku Ahoy does not operate its own advertising profile database.

Advertising data is handled under Google’s policies. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy.

4. Consent and advertising choices

Where required, consent choices are presented using Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can use the options shown in the consent flow to manage eligible advertising and privacy choices. Availability of particular choices may vary by region and applicable law.

5. In-app purchases

Sudoku Ahoy offers an optional non-consumable in-app purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card number or other full payment credentials. Apple may provide purchase status information needed to unlock or restore the purchased feature.

6. Tracking

Sudoku Ahoy does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access IDFA for cross-app tracking. We do not use data collected by Sudoku Ahoy to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. Google and its partners process advertising data according to your region, device settings, consent choices, and their own policies.

7. Information we do not sell

We do not sell your personal information. We do not operate a data brokerage business and do not rent personal information to third parties.

8. Children

Sudoku Ahoy is a general-audience puzzle game and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and consent behavior may vary by region and applicable law.

9. Data retention and security

Local gameplay data remains on your device unless removed by you, the operating system, or by deleting the app. Information processed by third-party services such as Google or Apple is retained according to their respective policies. We take reasonable steps to keep the app and its integrations secure, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your choices

  • You can remove the app to delete locally stored gameplay data.
  • You can manage Apple privacy and advertising settings from your device settings.
  • Where a consent flow is required, you can make the choices presented in that flow.
  • You may purchase Remove Ads to stop in-app advertising after the purchase is successfully recognized.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its third-party services, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are published.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Sudoku Ahoy, contact us at quietbyte188@gmail.com.
