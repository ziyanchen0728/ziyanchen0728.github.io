Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Sudoku Ahoy handles information when you use the app. Sudoku Ahoy is a logic puzzle game. We do not operate an account system and we do not ask you to provide a name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location in order to play.

2. Gameplay data

Gameplay information such as puzzle progress and settings may be stored locally on your device so the app can remember your progress and preferences. We do not intentionally upload your puzzle progress to our own servers.

3. Advertising

Sudoku Ahoy uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, general device information, ad interactions, and approximate location inferred from network information, depending on your region, device settings, and consent choices.

Where required, consent choices are presented using Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Advertising data is handled under Google’s own policies. You can learn more at Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases

Sudoku Ahoy offers an optional in-app purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card number or other full payment credentials. Apple may provide purchase status information needed to unlock or restore the purchased feature.

5. Information we do not sell

We do not sell your personal information. We do not operate a data brokerage business and do not rent personal information to third parties.

6. Children

Sudoku Ahoy is a general-audience puzzle game and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and consent behavior may vary by region and applicable law.

7. Data retention and security

Local gameplay data remains on your device unless removed by you, the operating system, or by deleting the app. Information processed by third-party services such as Google or Apple is retained according to their respective policies. We take reasonable steps to keep the app and its integrations secure, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices

  • You can remove the app to delete locally stored app data.
  • You can manage Apple privacy and advertising settings from your device settings.
  • Where a consent flow is required, you can make the choices presented in that flow.
  • You may purchase Remove Ads to stop in-app advertising in the app after the purchase is successfully recognized.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its third-party services, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are published.
