Support

Need help with a puzzle, an in-app purchase, ads, or a technical issue? The information below covers the most common questions.

Gameplay help

Each puzzle mode includes its own rules and controls. If you get stuck, use the in-game hint feature where available, or reset the current puzzle and try again.

Remove Ads purchase

  • Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.
  • If you previously purchased Remove Ads, use the app’s restore purchase option on the same Apple Account.
  • If a charge appears but the purchase is not unlocked, restart the app and try restoring purchases before contacting support.

Ads and consent

Sudoku Ahoy uses Google Mobile Ads. Where required, a consent message may be shown before personalized advertising or related data processing. Availability and ad content can vary by region.

Contact

When reporting a problem, please include your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful when possible.
